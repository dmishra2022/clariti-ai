Services

Custom AI, built around how you actually make decisions — not a bolt-on chatbot.

We work end to end: strategy, product design, model and agent engineering, and the unglamorous integration work that decides whether an AI system survives contact with your real data.

01 · BUILD

Custom GenAI Application Development

LLM-powered copilots, internal tools and customer-facing assistants designed around your workflows, tone and data — not a wrapper on a generic model.

  • Domain-grounded copilots and assistants
  • Retrieval systems over your internal knowledge
  • Workflow automation inside tools you already use
02 · DECIDE

Decision Intelligence Systems

Autonomous, explainable systems for pricing, demand forecasting, inventory and risk — built with the same discipline behind our own platform, Decisio.

  • Price elasticity and margin modelling
  • Multi-model demand forecasting
  • Inventory and reorder planning
03 · ACT

AI Agent Engineering

Goal-oriented agents with memory, tool access and configurable approval workflows, so autonomy is earned gradually and never unchecked.

  • Human-in-the-loop approval design
  • Tool use, function calling and guardrails
  • Confidence scoring and escalation paths
04 · CONNECT

Data & Systems Integration

Your ERP, CRM and warehouse hold the truth your AI needs. We connect them into one reliable layer instead of another silo.

  • ERP, CRM and POS integrations
  • Real-time and batch data pipelines
  • Clean, versioned data contracts
05 · ADVISE

AI Strategy & Advisory

Roadmapping, feasibility assessment, model selection and responsible-AI governance — before a single line of code, if that's what's needed.

  • Use-case prioritisation and ROI modelling
  • Build vs. buy and model selection
  • Governance, risk and approval frameworks
06 · OPERATE

Managed AI Operations

Systems drift as your business changes. We monitor, retrain and extend what we build so performance holds long after launch.

  • Model and drift monitoring
  • Scheduled retraining and evaluation
  • Ongoing feature and integration support
